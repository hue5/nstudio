Wedding and portrait photography. By appointment.

hello@nikhilstudio.example

A small studio, a long look.

A practice built on patience, natural light, and the frame nobody planned.

The best photographs are usually the ones nobody planned.

We keep the room quiet and the directions short. A wedding is not a photoshoot. It is a day, and we photograph it that way.

Portraits happen best when you forget the camera is there. We talk, walk, and wait for the face you make when you are not performing.

The work

Details, faces, and the light between them. We keep the studio simple so the pictures can be about you.

How we work

Three parts, no performance.

01 / 03

  1. Before

    We talk through the day and the pictures you actually want. No long shot lists, no performance.

  2. During

    Natural light, few directions, and enough time for you to forget the camera is there.

  3. After

    A first look, then the full gallery, then prints if you want them. Kept simple, kept yours.
