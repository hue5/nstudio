How a day runs

No performance, no long shot lists. A simple plan and room to breathe.

01 / 03

  1. A conversation

    We meet before the date, talk through the day, and make a simple plan. Where the light falls, when to eat, who matters most.

  2. The day itself

    We arrive early, stay out of the way, and follow what is already happening. Few directions, long looks, no forced smiles.

  3. After

    A first look soon after, then the full gallery, then prints if you want them. Kept simple, kept yours.
